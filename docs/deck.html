Three counting failures. Lead with the numbers, not the concept. 400 activity records is 138 molecules. Why the choice is graded “inferred”. That UP4 contains the core is computed and reproducible. That 6X48 is therefore the right receptor is a conclusion — no database states it, so the citation check has nothing to re-fetch. The grade is how the dossier tells a reader which line to check themselves.
The medicinal chemistry heart of the project, and the slide a chemist in the room will actually engage with. Say it plainly: we recommend the blurrier structure. 6X48 at 2.9 Å over 5MRI at 2.00 Å, because the ligand bound in 6X48 contains the core our series is built on. The pocket is already holding our chemotype, side chains and all — an induced fit we would otherwise be guessing at. Then the grading point. The substructure match is computed and reproducible. The conclusion that follows from it is not, so it is graded inferred. No database states "this is the right receptor", so the citation check has nothing to re-fetch. That distinction is what makes the dossier checkable rather than merely confident.
step 4the five answers, and what each one decides3:00 – 3:45

Five questions, then stop

1Which structure do we design against? one named entry with a reason, not a shortlist → resolution + ligand checks
2What chemical matter exists? distinct molecules and potency range — never the record count → series-depth check
3Is there a congeneric series? 138 unrelated singletons teach you nothing. 106 analogs on one core give you an SAR gradient — the thing design actually optimises against → series-depth check
4Is the field still active? context for a reader: a target nobody has published on for a decade is worth flagging, but silence is not evidence about the protein → gates nothing
5What is missing? every failed search and every demoted claim, listed even when empty → the verdict
Question 3 is the one that decides most campaigns, and question 4 is the one that decides nothing — it is reported, not weighed. Saying which is which is part of the deliverable.
Five questions, and what each one feeds. Question 3 is the one that decides most campaigns. 138 unrelated singletons and 106 analogs on one core are the same compound count and completely different projects — only one of them gives you an SAR gradient to optimise against. the honest caveat4:35 – 5:00

Tractable is not the same as working

Sortilin is the clearance receptor for progranulin. Blocking it raises progranulin — which is exactly what an anti-sortilin antibody did in patients. It moved the biomarker and still missed its Phase 3 endpoint.

what it answerswhether the chemistry can start: a pocket, a series, and which structure to use
what it does notwhether the biology pays out — no synthesis, no assay, no docking, no free energy
